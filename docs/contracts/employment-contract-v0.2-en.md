## 2. REMUNERATION

### 2.1 Pay Model by Level

| Level | Pay Model | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| Probation (0–3 months) | Hourly | `$[PROBATION_HOURLY]`/hour |
| Cleaning Technician | Hourly | `$[TECHNICIAN_HOURLY]`/hour |
| Senior Technician | Day Rate | `$[SENIOR_DAY_RATE]`/day |
| Team Leader | Day Rate + Differential | `$[TEAM_LEADER_DAY_RATE]`/day |

### 2.2 Hourly Pay (Probation and Technician)

2.2.1 The Employee is paid for all hours worked, including service time and travel time between service locations. Overtime is payable at 1.5× the hourly rate for hours over eight (8) in a day or forty (40) in a week, and at 2× for hours over twelve (12) in a day, per sections 35–40 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC).

2.2.2 **Minimum Daily Hours.** If the Employee reports to work as scheduled and work is not available, the Employee shall be paid for a minimum of two (2) hours, per section 34 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC).

### 2.3 Day Rate (Senior Technician and Team Leader)

2.3.1 The day rate covers a workday of up to eight (8) hours, including service time and travel between service locations. The Employee shall never be required or permitted to work more than eight (8) hours in a day.

2.3.2 The day rate is the same regardless of the number of services completed or the actual hours worked, provided the hours do not exceed eight (8). A day with fewer than eight (8) hours is paid at the full day rate — this rewards efficiency and quality.

2.3.3 **Minimum Wage Floor.** The effective hourly rate (Day Rate ÷ 8) shall never fall below the BC minimum wage. If the minimum wage increases above this floor, the day rate shall be adjusted upward at the next pay period.

2.3.4 **No Overtime for Day Rate.** Day-rate employees do not work more than eight (8) hours per day. If a service requires additional time, the Company shall send additional personnel or schedule a second day. Under no circumstances shall a day-rate employee work overtime.

2.3.5 **Day Without Services.** If the Employee is scheduled and reports to work but no services are available, the Employee shall be paid for a minimum of two (2) hours at the hourly rate equivalent (Day Rate ÷ 8 × 2).

### 2.4 Pay Period

Wages are paid semi-monthly (15th and last day of each month) via direct deposit. Each pay shall be accompanied by a wage statement detailing gross wages, deductions, and net pay, per section 27 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC).

### 2.5 Annual IPC Adjustment

The hourly rate and day rate shall be reviewed annually on the anniversary of the Start Date. The rate shall be adjusted by the BC Consumer Price Index (CPI), All-Items, as published by Statistics Canada. The adjusted rate = Current Rate × (1 + CPI change%). The adjustment shall never reduce the rate below the previous year's rate. The Employee shall receive written notice of any adjustment at least thirty (30) days before the effective date.

### 2.6 Parking

The Company provides a corporate payment card for work-related parking. The Team Leader is responsible for the card during assigned shifts. Parking charges are automatically recorded. Personal use is prohibited. Employees at the hourly level shall submit parking receipts for reimbursement. The Company is not responsible for parking fines or tickets incurred by the Employee.

## 3. HOURS OF WORK

3.1 **Workday.** A standard workday is up to eight (8) hours, typically between Monday and Friday. Actual start and end times vary based on client scheduling and travel between service locations. The dispatch system (`dispatch-team.ts`, `schedule-7030.ts`) determines daily assignments.

3.2 **Schedule Notification.** The Employee shall receive their schedule at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance via the PWA.

3.3 **Meal Break.** The Employee is entitled to an unpaid thirty (30) minute meal break after every five (5) consecutive hours of work, per section 32 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC). The exact timing depends on the day's service schedule and is displayed in the PWA. The Employee may take the meal break in the Company vehicle. During the break, the Employee is fully relieved of all duties — no driving, no client communication, no equipment handling.

3.4 **Hours Free From Work.** The Employee shall have at least eight (8) consecutive hours free from work between shifts, per section 36 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC). The system (`shift-rest.ts`) enforces this requirement.

3.5 **Maximum Hours.** No Employee shall be required or permitted to work excessive hours that could endanger health or safety. The system (`workday.ts`) enforces schedule compliance. Day-rate employees shall never exceed eight (8) hours per day.

---

## 4. TRAVEL AND VEHICLE

4.1 **Travel Between Services.** Travel time between service locations during the workday is work time. For hourly employees, travel time is paid at the regular hourly rate. For day-rate employees, travel time is included in the day rate.

4.2 **Commute to First Service / From Last Service.** The Employee's commute from home to the first service location and from the last service location to home is not work time and is not compensated.

4.3 **Company Vehicle.** If assigned a Company vehicle, the Employee must hold a valid BC driver's licence and maintain a clean driving record. The vehicle is for work use only. The Employee shall report any damage, accident, or mechanical issue immediately. Personal use requires prior written authorization.

4.4 **Personal Vehicle.** If the Employee uses a personal vehicle for work travel (with prior written authorization), the Company shall reimburse at the CRA mileage rate. The Employee must maintain adequate personal auto insurance and notify their insurer of business use.

## 5. STATUTORY HOLIDAYS

5.1 **Recognized Holidays.** The Company observes the eleven (11) statutory holidays of British Columbia: New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, B.C. Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving Day, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day.

5.2 **Holiday Pay.** An eligible Employee who does not work on a statutory holiday receives an average day's pay. An eligible Employee who works on a statutory holiday receives 1.5× the regular rate for hours worked, plus an average day's pay, per Part 5 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC).

5.3 **Eligibility.** The Employee must have been employed for at least thirty (30) calendar days and must have worked or earned wages on at least fifteen (15) of the thirty (30) days preceding the holiday.

## 6. VACATION

6.1 **Annual Entitlement.** The Employee is entitled to:
- **Two (2) weeks** of vacation after each of the first five (5) years (vacation pay at 4% of gross wages)
- **Three (3) weeks** after five (5) consecutive years (vacation pay at 6% of gross wages)

6.2 **Vacation Pay.** Vacation pay is accrued on all wages and may be paid on each paycheque or taken as paid time off, at the Employee's election communicated in writing.

6.3 **Scheduling.** Vacation must be requested at least four (4) weeks in advance and is subject to operational requirements. The Company shall not unreasonably withhold approval.

## 12. DUTIES AND CONDUCT

12.1 **Core Duties.** The Employee's core duties include:
- Performing cleaning services according to the Company's Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) by zone
- Following the chemical lockout protocol: using only Company-approved products and concentrations
- Wearing required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) at all times during service
- Taking timestamped, geotagged completion photos of each serviced zone through the PWA
- Participating in pre-service briefing and end-of-shift ritual (`shift-ritual.ts`)
- Maintaining the Company's equipment and vehicle in clean, operational condition
- Reporting any client concern, incident, or damage immediately via the PWA

12.2 **Chemical and Safety Protocol.** The Employee shall adhere strictly to the Company's chemical lockout system. The Employee shall not use any cleaning product not explicitly approved in the system. Safety Data Sheets (SDS) for all approved products are available in the PWA.

12.3 **Environmental Compliance.** The Employee shall dispose of cleaning wastewater through sanitary drains only (not storm drains). Any spill of concentrated product exceeding four (4) litres must be reported immediately to the supervisor.

12.4 **Key Handling.** If entrusted with client keys or alarm codes, the Employee must follow the key handling protocol (`key-handling.ts`). Lost keys or security breaches must be reported within thirty (30) minutes of discovery. Keys and codes are stored and transmitted only through the encrypted Company system.

12.5 **Photo Evidence.** The Employee shall capture and upload timestamped, geotagged photos of each zone after completion. These photos serve as the Company's primary quality assurance and dispute resolution evidence.

12.6 **Client Confidentiality.** The Employee shall not, during or after employment, disclose to any third party any information about the Company's clients, including but not limited to:

(a) Client names, addresses, and contact information
(b) Property details, layout, security features, or contents
(c) Security information: keys, alarm codes, lockbox codes, access codes
(d) Personal circumstances, family composition, schedules, or habits of any household member
(e) Photographs or videos taken inside any client property, for any purpose other than the Company's required work documentation through the PWA system
(f) Any observation made while on a client's property

**Prohibited conduct includes but is not limited to:**
- Posting about clients or client properties on social media
- Sharing client information with friends, family, or other clients
- Using client information for personal gain
- Retaining client information after employment ends

**Consequences of Breach.** Breach of this clause constitutes just cause for immediate termination under Section 14.2. The Employee acknowledges that a breach of confidentiality may also result in personal civil liability to the affected client and/or criminal liability under applicable law.

**Survival.** This clause survives termination of employment indefinitely.

12.7 **No Independent Work.** During the term of this Agreement, the Employee shall not perform cleaning services directly for any Company client outside of the Company's booking and dispatch system.

12.8 **Trespass and Safety Protocol.** If a client revokes access to the Property, becomes hostile, or the Employee reasonably fears for safety, the Employee shall immediately: (a) stop work, (b) leave the premises, (c) contact the supervisor, and (d) document the incident. No disciplinary action shall be taken for leaving an unsafe situation. The Employee shall not remain on the Property against the client's instructions under section 4 of the *Trespass Act* (BC).

12.9 **Employee Liability for Damage.**

(a) **Accidental Damage.** The Employee shall report any damage to client property immediately through the PWA incident system. For accidental damage occurring within the scope of employment and while following the Company's SOPs, the Company's insurance covers the loss and the Employee shall not be personally liable for the cost of repair or the insurance deductible.

(b) **Gross Negligence or Willful Misconduct.** For damage caused by gross negligence (failure to follow SOPs after documented training) or willful misconduct, the Company may seek contribution from the Employee up to a maximum of `$[DAMAGE_CAP]` CAD.

(c) **Outside Scope of Employment.** Any damage caused by the Employee acting outside the scope of employment (including theft, intentional damage, or criminal acts) is the Employee's personal responsibility.

## 14. TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT

14.1 **Termination by the Company (Without Just Cause).** The Company may terminate this Agreement without just cause by providing the following notice or pay in lieu, per section 63 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC):

| Length of Employment | Notice or Pay in Lieu |
|---|---|
| Less than 3 months | 1 week (probation per Section 1.3) |
| 3 months to 1 year | 1 week |
| 1 to 3 years | 2 weeks |
| 3 to 5 years | 3 weeks |
| 5 to 10 years | 4 weeks |
| 10+ years | 5 to 8 weeks (per ESA formula) |

14.2 **Termination for Just Cause.** The Company may terminate this Agreement immediately without notice or pay in lieu for just cause, including but not limited to: theft, fraud, gross insubordination, wilful misconduct, breach of confidentiality (Section 12.6), violation of the chemical lockout or safety protocol, or abandonment of position.

14.3 **Termination by the Employee.** The Employee may resign by providing written notice of: one (1) week if employed less than two (2) years; two (2) weeks if employed two (2) years or more.

14.4 **Final Pay.** Upon termination, the Company shall pay all outstanding wages, accrued vacation pay, and any other amounts owing within the time required by section 18 of the *Employment Standards Act* (BC).

14.5 **Return of Property.** Upon termination, the Employee shall immediately return all Company property — including keys, access cards, uniforms, devices, equipment, corporate payment cards, and any documents (physical or digital) — in good condition, reasonable wear excepted.
