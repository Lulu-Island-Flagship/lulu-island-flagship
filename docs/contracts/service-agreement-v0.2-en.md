# PART A — GENERAL TERMS (APPLIES TO ALL SERVICES)

## A.1 SERVICE SCOPE

A.1.1 **Property.** The cleaning service shall be performed at the address stated in SCHEDULE A (the "Property").

A.1.2 **BC Assessment Area.** The Property's floor area, as recorded by BC Assessment, is the basis for pricing. This figure is obtained from the provincial database and is objectively verifiable.

A.1.3 **Service Specifications.** The service type, zones included, add-ons, IES level, organic load factors, team size, scheduled date, and arrival window are set out in SCHEDULE A.

A.1.4 **What Is Included.** The specific tasks included vary by service type and are detailed in SCHEDULE A. Each zone receives the Company's Standard Operating Procedure appropriate to the service type.

A.1.5 **What Is NOT Included.** Unless selected as an add-on: interior of appliances, interior of cabinets, exterior windows above ground floor, furniture over 25 kg, and items in Section A.8.

## A.2 FIXED PRICE AND TAX

A.2.1 **Fixed Price.** The total price is stated in SCHEDULE A. Fixed and upfront. No hourly rate.

A.2.2 **GST/HST.** Included in total. Company GST/HST: `[GST_NUMBER]`. Excise Tax Act (Canada).

A.2.3 **No Hidden Charges.** No fuel, travel, or product surcharge after booking.

A.2.4 **Quotation Validity.** Written quotes valid for thirty (30) days.

## A.3 PAYMENT

A.3.1 **Hold and Batch Capture.** Hold at booking; capture within 24h after QC approval.

A.3.2 **Payment Method.** Stripe. Card details never on Company servers.

A.3.3 **B2B Invoicing.** CRA-compliant tax invoice after each service: Company name/address/GST, Billing Party name/address/GST, sequential invoice number, dates, description, total, GST separately. Due in 30 days. B2C: Stripe receipt serves as record.

A.3.4 **Declined Payment.** 48h to resolve, then Section A.11 applies.

A.3.5 **Installment Option.** If total > `[INSTALLMENT_THRESHOLD]` CAD and selected, split per SCHEDULE A.

## A.4 CANCELLATION AND RESCHEDULING

A.4.1 **Cancellation by Billing Party.** 48h notice = no charge. Via Client Portal or direct contact.

A.4.2 **Late Cancellation Fee.** Less than 48h: `$[LATE_CANCELLATION_FEE]` or `[LATE_CANCELLATION_PERCENT]%`, whichever less.

A.4.3 **No-Show.** Team arrives, cannot access = late cancellation.

A.4.4 **Cancellation by Company.** Weather, equipment, staff, safety. No charge. Priority rescheduling.

A.4.5 **Cooling-Off.** Agreement formed online. If cooling-off applies, Billing Party consents to immediate commencement by scheduling within that period.

## A.5 WARRANTY AND QUALITY ASSURANCE

A.5.1 **Photo Evidence Guarantee.** Each zone photographed after completion. Photos are sole evidence.

A.5.2 **Warranty Claim.** Within 24h via survey or Portal. Evaluated against timestamped photos.

A.5.3 **Remedy.** (a) Re-clean at no charge within `[REMEDY_WINDOW]` days, or (b) credit proportional to zone weight. Limit: one claim per service.

A.5.4 **Refunds.** Primary remedy is re-clean or credit. Cash refund where required by law, proportional to work not performed to standard. No refund for completed, photographed, SOP-compliant work.

A.5.5 **RTA Standard.** For move-out services, RTA s.32 "reasonable health, cleanliness and sanitary standards" is the benchmark. Below-standard pre-existing conditions do not entitle claims.

## A.6 ACCESS TO PROPERTY

A.6.1 **Access Methods.** (a) Adult present, (b) key on file (encrypted), (c) lockbox/door code (encrypted).

A.6.2 **Key Security.** Non-identifying codes. Encrypted at rest and in transit.

A.6.3 **Pets.** Service Recipient shall secure pets. Company not responsible for escape or interference.

A.6.4 **Trespass Protocol.** Booking = implied license (Trespass Act s.4). Service Recipient may revoke at any time. If revoked: team stops, documents, departs. Billing Party pays proportion completed. Team not required to stay.

A.6.5 **Safety to Leave.** Threatening/abusive/unsafe behavior = team may leave immediately. Bill proportion completed. Document reason.

## A.7 CHEMICAL LOCKOUT AND PRODUCTS

A.7.1 **Company-Approved Only.** Chemical lockout system. SDS available.

A.7.2 **No Client Products.** Company shall not use client-provided products.

A.7.3 **Allergies.** Disclose during booking. Substitute within approved catalogue where feasible.

## A.8 EXCLUSIONS

A.8.1 Explicitly excluded: Biohazard (IES 5), mould/mildew remediation, pest infestation, hoarding, exterior windows (no safe access), furniture >25 kg, inside appliances (unless add-on).

A.8.2 If excluded condition discovered: notify, pause, refer specialist. No late fee if Service Recipient could not reasonably have known.

## A.9 LIABILITY AND INSURANCE

A.9.1 **Insurance.** Commercial general liability. WorkSafeBC registered. Certificate on request.

A.9.2 **Pre-Existing Damage.** Not responsible. Documented on arrival.

A.9.3 **Incidental Damage.** Notify, document, repair/replace up to insurance limit.

A.9.4 **Valuables.** Service Recipient shall secure valuables. Liability limited to insurance recovery.

A.9.5 **Limitation of Liability.** To maximum extent permitted by law (including BPCPA), total liability ≤ total price paid for that service, except personal injury/property damage from Company negligence = limited to insurance coverage.

A.9.6 **Occupiers Liability.** Service Recipient owes duty of care (Occupiers Liability Act): safe premises, no tripping hazards, secure pets, warn of dangers. Company owes reciprocal duty during service.

A.9.7 **Vicarious Liability.** Company responsible for employee acts within scope of employment. NOT responsible for: criminal acts, intentional damage/theft, confidentiality breach outside scope, personal-purpose acts — provided Company took reasonable hiring/training/supervision steps. Company cooperates with law enforcement.

A.10.1 **Collection.** Service delivery, communication, payment, legal compliance. PIPA (BC).

A.10.2 **Sensitive Information.** Access codes/keys encrypted at rest. Team leader access only during service.

A.10.5 **Retention.** Seven (7) years after last service. Destroyed thereafter, subject to Limitation Act (2 years).

A.10.8 **Company Reasonable Steps.** (a) Signed confidentiality clause, (b) onboarding training, (c) PWA restricts photos, (d) breaches investigated and disciplined up to termination.

A.10.9 **Limitation for Employee Breach.** Company not liable where employee acted outside scope and Company took reasonable steps. Employee personally liable.
